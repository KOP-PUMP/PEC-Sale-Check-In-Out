# pec_checkin

## Description

The PEC Check-in application is a powerful tool designed specifically for our employee to streamline attendance tracking and reporting. With this app, user can easily check in and out at their customer with precise location and time stamps, ensuring accurate record-keeping for HR. The app also features an integrated customer list directly retrieved from the company's database, allowing employees to seamlessly report attendance to their supervisors. The app automatically summarizes check-in/check-out data within a specified time frame, providing HR with concise reports for performance evaluation.

## Device Compatibility

### Optimized for iPhone

This app is specifically designed and optimized for iPhone devices. The UI, features, and overall experience are tailored for iPhone screen sizes and resolutions.

### Running on iPad

While the app can be installed and run on iPad devices, it will operate in compatibility mode, which presents the app as it would appear on an iPhone screen. Please note that:

- The app is not optimized for the larger screen sizes of iPads.
- You may notice black borders around the app, as it is displayed at the iPhone's aspect ratio.
- Functionality remains consistent with the iPhone experience, but visual elements may not fully utilize the iPad screen space.

### Recommendation

For the best user experience, we recommend using this app on an iPhone. If running on an iPad, please be aware that the app is in compatibility mode and may not take full advantage of the iPad’s screen size and features.

## Features
- **Precise Check-In/Out:** Capture accurate location and time for check-ins and check-outs.
- **Customer List Integration:** Retrieve customer data directly from the company database.
- **Automated Reporting:** Generate attendance summaries within specified time frames for HR review.
- **Supervisor Notifications:** Allow Sales Engineers to easily report attendance to their supervisors.
- **Secure Data Handling:** Ensure secure storage and transmission of sensitive data.

## Usage
- **Check-In/Out:** Open the app and tap on the 'Locate' button (blue button) to precisely locate your real time location and tap 'Check-in' button (green button). The app will automatically record your location and time. To check out, repeat the same step with 'Check-out' (red button)
- **Select Customer:** Choose a customer from the integrated list to associate with your check-in/check-out.
- **View Summary:** Access your attendance summary and report it directly to your supervisor through the app.

## Configuration
- **Tracking:** Ensure application tracking services are enabled on your device to use the check-in/check-out functionality.
- **Location Services:** Ensure location services are enabled on your device to use the check-in/check-out functionality.
